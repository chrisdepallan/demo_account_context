# Members and Roles

## How do I invite a member?

Owners and Admins go to **Settings → Members → Invite**, enter one or more email addresses (up to **50 per batch**) and choose a role. Invitations expire after **7 days** and can be resent.

## Who can do what?

| Action | Owner | Admin | Billing admin | Member | Guest |
|--------|-------|-------|---------------|--------|-------|
| Create and edit projects | Yes | Yes | No | Yes | No |
| Comment | Yes | Yes | No | Yes | Yes |
| Invite / remove members | Yes | Yes | No | No | No |
| Change security settings (SSO, MFA policy) | Yes | Yes | No | No | No |
| View and pay invoices | Yes | No | Yes | No | No |
| Change plan or payment method | Yes | No | Yes | No | No |
| Transfer ownership | Yes | No | No | No | No |
| Close the account | Yes | No | No | No | No |

## How do I remove a member?

Owners and Admins go to **Settings → Members**, choose the member and select **Remove**. The member loses access immediately. Content they created stays in the workspace and is reassigned to the person who removed them. The freed seat is credited on the next invoice, prorated by day.

## How do I transfer account ownership?

The Owner goes to **Settings → Account → Transfer ownership** and picks an existing Admin. The new Owner must accept within **7 days**. After transfer, the previous Owner becomes an Admin.

If the Owner has left the company and cannot be reached, an Admin can contact support. Support needs written confirmation from a company officer on company letterhead, and the process takes up to **5 business days**.

## Do guests cost money?

No. Each paid seat allows 5 free guests. For example, an account with 10 seats can have up to 50 guests.
