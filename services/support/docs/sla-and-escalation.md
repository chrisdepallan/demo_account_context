# Support SLAs and Escalation

## Which support channels are available on each plan?

| Channel | Free | Starter | Pro | Business | Enterprise |
|---------|------|---------|-----|----------|------------|
| Help center and community forum | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Email | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Live chat (business hours) | No | No | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Phone | No | No | No | Yes | Yes |
| 24/7 for P1 incidents | No | No | No | No | Yes |
| Named Customer Success Manager | No | No | No | No | Yes |

## How are priorities defined?

- **P1 – Critical**: service down or data loss for many users, no workaround.
- **P2 – High**: a major feature is broken for many users, or a workaround is very hard.
- **P3 – Normal**: a feature is broken for some users, or a workaround exists.
- **P4 – Low**: questions, how-to requests and feature requests.

## What are the first response times?

| Priority | Free | Starter | Pro | Business | Enterprise |
|----------|------|---------|-----|----------|------------|
| P1 | Best effort | 24 business hours | 8 business hours | 2 hours | 30 minutes, 24/7 |
| P2 | Best effort | 48 business hours | 12 business hours | 4 business hours | 2 hours, 24/7 |
| P3 | Best effort | 3 business days | 1 business day | 8 business hours | 4 business hours |
| P4 | Best effort | 5 business days | 2 business days | 1 business day | 1 business day |

Business hours are Monday–Friday, 08:00–18:00 US Central Time.

## How are tickets escalated?

1. **Tier 1** (frontline) handles the ticket first.
2. If not solved within **4 business hours** for P2 or **1 business day** for P3, it goes to **Tier 2** (product specialists).
3. Confirmed bugs go to the owning engineering team through a Jira issue linked to the ticket.
4. P1 tickets page the on-call engineer immediately and open an incident on the status page within **15 minutes**.

## Can a customer ask for an escalation?

Yes. The customer can reply "escalate" on the ticket, or Business and Enterprise customers can call the support line. The ticket is reviewed by a team lead within **1 business day**. Enterprise customers can also contact their Customer Success Manager directly.

## How long are tickets kept?

Closed tickets are kept for **3 years**, then anonymized. A ticket closed by mistake can be reopened by replying within **14 days**; after that a new ticket is created.

## What should support never do?

- Never ask for or accept a customer's password, full card number or MFA code.
- Never reset MFA or change the account Owner based on email alone.
- Never share one customer's data with another, even within the same company, unless they are members of the same workspace.
