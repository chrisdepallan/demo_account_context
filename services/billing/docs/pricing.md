# Pricing

## List prices

All prices are in US dollars, per seat, excluding tax.

| Plan | Monthly billing | Annual billing (per month) | Annual total per seat |
|------|-----------------|----------------------------|-----------------------|
| Free | $0 | $0 | $0 |
| Starter | $8 | $6.67 | $80 |
| Pro | $15 | $12.50 | $150 |
| Business | $29 | $24.17 | $290 |
| Enterprise | Custom | Custom | Custom (min. 50 seats) |

## How does annual billing save money?

Annual billing charges 10 months for 12 months of service, which is 2 months free (about 16.7% off).

## Are prices available in other currencies?

Yes. Accounts with a billing address in the EU are billed in EUR, in the UK in GBP, and in India in INR. Prices are set per currency and do not follow daily exchange rates:

| Plan | USD | EUR | GBP | INR |
|------|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| Starter | $8 | €8 | £7 | ₹549 |
| Pro | $15 | €14 | £12 | ₹999 |
| Business | $29 | €27 | £24 | ₹1,999 |

The billing currency is set at the first payment and can only be changed by support.

## Discounts

- **Nonprofits**: 50% off Starter, Pro and Business with proof of registered nonprofit status.
- **Education**: Pro is free for verified students and teachers for up to 10 seats.
- **Startups**: 30% off for the first 12 months for companies under 2 years old with less than $5M in funding.

Only one discount can apply at a time. Discounts do not apply to Enterprise.

## How do seat changes affect my bill?

- **Adding seats**: charged immediately, prorated by day for the rest of the cycle.
- **Removing seats**: credited on the next invoice, prorated by day. No cash refund.

## When will prices change?

Acme Cloud gives at least **60 days' notice** by email before any price increase. Annual customers keep their current price until their next renewal.
