# Invoices and Taxes

## Where do I find my invoices?

The Owner and Billing admins can download all invoices as PDF from **Settings → Billing → Invoices**. Invoices are also emailed to the billing email address on every charge. Invoices are kept for **7 years**.

## Can I change the details on an invoice?

Yes. Company name, billing address, VAT/GST ID and PO number can be updated in **Settings → Billing → Billing details**. Changes apply to future invoices. To reissue a past invoice with new details, contact support; invoices from the last **12 months** can be reissued.

## How do I add a billing email address?

Up to **5 extra billing email addresses** can be added under **Settings → Billing → Billing contacts**. They receive invoices and payment alerts but cannot log in unless they are also members.

## Do you charge US sales tax?

Yes, in states where Acme Cloud is required to collect it. The rate is based on the billing address and shown on each invoice. Tax-exempt organizations can email their exemption certificate to `billing@acme-cloud.example`; exemption applies from the next invoice and is not applied retroactively.

## Do you charge VAT?

- **EU businesses** with a valid VAT ID: no VAT is charged (reverse charge). The VAT ID is validated against VIES.
- **EU customers without a VAT ID**: VAT is charged at the rate of the customer's country (for example 19% in Germany, 20% in France, 21% in the Netherlands).
- **UK**: 20% VAT unless a valid UK VAT number is provided.

## Do you charge GST in India?

Yes. 18% GST is charged on all Indian accounts. Businesses can add their GSTIN to claim input tax credit.

## Can I get a quote or pro-forma invoice before paying?

Yes, for annual plans. Sales can issue a pro-forma invoice valid for **30 days**. Contact `sales@acme-cloud.example`.
