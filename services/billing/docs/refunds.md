# Refunds

## What is the refund policy?

| Situation | Refund |
|-----------|--------|
| Annual plan, cancelled within 30 days of purchase or renewal | Full refund |
| Annual plan, cancelled after 30 days | No refund; service continues until the end of the term |
| Monthly plan | No refund for the current month; cancel to stop the next charge |
| Seats removed mid-cycle | Prorated credit on the next invoice, not a cash refund |
| Charged after cancelling (our error) | Full refund of the incorrect charge |
| Duplicate charge | Full refund of the duplicate |
| Outage breaching the uptime SLA | Service credit (see below), not a cash refund |

## How long do refunds take?

Refunds are issued within **2 business days** of approval. They then take **5–10 business days** to appear on the customer's card statement, depending on the bank. Bank transfer refunds take up to **10 business days**.

## Where does the refund go?

Always to the original payment method. If the card has expired or been closed, the bank normally forwards the refund to the replacement card. If it bounces, support can issue the refund as account credit or by bank transfer.

## Who can approve a refund?

| Amount | Approver |
|--------|----------|
| Up to $500 | Any support agent |
| $500.01 – $5,000 | Support team lead |
| Over $5,000 | Billing manager |

Refunds outside the policy (goodwill refunds) need team-lead approval at any amount and are limited to one per account per 12 months.

## How do I issue a refund? (support agents)

1. Open the account in the admin console and check the invoice is paid and inside policy.
2. Select **Invoices → Refund**, choose full or partial and add the ticket number in the reason field.
3. The refund appears as `pending` and moves to `succeeded` once the processor confirms.
4. Reply to the customer with the refund amount and the 5–10 business day timeline.

## SLA service credits

If monthly uptime falls below the plan's SLA, the customer can request a credit within **30 days** of the end of that month:

| Monthly uptime (Business plan, 99.95% SLA) | Credit |
|--------------------------------------------|--------|
| 99.0% – 99.95% | 10% of monthly fee |
| 95.0% – 99.0% | 25% of monthly fee |
| Below 95.0% | 50% of monthly fee |

Credits are capped at 50% of that month's fee and are applied to the next invoice.

## Chargebacks

If a customer files a chargeback instead of asking for a refund, the account is moved to `read_only` until the dispute is resolved. A $15 dispute fee may be charged if the chargeback is found invalid.
